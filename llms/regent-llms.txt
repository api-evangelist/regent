# REGENT Craft Inc.

> REGENT is pioneering the future of maritime mobility through the development and manufacturing of Seaglider™ vessels, high-speed hydrofoiling wing-in-ground effect (WIG) craft that operate over the water within a wingspan of the surface, combining the speed of an aircraft with the convenience of a boat. A dual use company based in Rhode Island, U.S., REGENT has secured global commercial orders valued at more than $10 billion from leading airline and ferry operators around the world, as well as $15 million in contracts with the U.S. Marine Corps. REGENT has raised more than $100 million from investors including 8090 Industries, Founders Fund, Japan Airlines, and Lockheed Martin.

## What is a Seaglider?

A Seaglider is a wing-in-ground-effect vehicle that operates exclusively over water in one of three modes: floating on its hull, hydrofoiling, or flying in ground effect just above the water's surface. This unique hybrid of aircraft and marine vessel achieves airplane-like speeds at boat-like costs, with zero emissions from its 100% battery-electric propulsion system. Seagliders use existing dock infrastructure and are built to the combined safety standards of modern aircraft and watercraft.

Key performance characteristics (Viceroy, flagship model):
- Cruise speed: 180 mph (160 knots / 300 kph)
- Range: 180 statute miles / 160 nautical miles with existing battery technology; upgradeable to 400+ nautical miles with next-generation batteries
- Capacity: 12 passengers + 2 crew (passenger configuration); 3,500 lbs payload (cargo configuration)
- Noise: 30 dB quieter than comparable aircraft or helicopters
- Emissions: Zero (100% battery electric)
- Cost: Approximately half the price of an aircraft ticket for equivalent routes

## Technology

### Wing-in-Ground Effect (WIG/Ground Effect)
Seagliders exploit the aerodynamic "ground effect" — increased lift and reduced drag that occurs when an aircraft flies within approximately one wingspan of a surface. By flying over water, Seagliders achieve aircraft-like efficiencies without the altitude or airspace complexity of traditional aviation.

### Three Operating Modes
1. **Hull mode**: Conventional boat hull for low-speed maneuvering in harbors and docking
2. **Hydrofoil mode**: Retractable hydrofoils lift the hull out of the water for high-speed water transit with reduced drag and smooth ride
3. **Ground effect flight**: Wings generate lift, hydrofoils retract, and the craft flies just above the water surface at up to 180 mph

### Autonomous Systems (Defense variants)
- Underwater perception: wildlife, obstacle, and threat detection below the surface
- Altitude & wave perception: determining height above water and forward wave state
- Surface traffic perception: identifying obstacles on the water's surface

### Safety
- Always within gliding distance of a safe water landing
- 12 redundant distributed electric motors
- Combined maritime and aerospace safety standards
- Avionics suite: synthetic vision, forward-looking sonar, maritime radar, IR camera, traffic advisory systems
